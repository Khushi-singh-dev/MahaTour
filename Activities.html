Maharashtra! One State, A Million Journeys

Adventure Tourism: White-water rafting at Kolad, skydiving in Aamby Valley/Dhule, hot air ballooning in Pune, and ziplining. Trekking & Hiking: Numerous treks covering 350+ forts in the Sahyadri range, including Harishchandragad, Torna, and Sinhagad, especially popular during monsoons. Water Sports & Beaches: Snorkeling and scuba diving in Tarkarli, along with popular beach spots like Murud and Ganpatipule. Wildlife & Eco-tourism: Safaris at Tadoba Andhari Tiger Reserve, Sanjay Gandhi National Park, and birdwatching at Bhigwan. Cultural & Heritage: Exploring UNESCO World Heritage sites (Ajanta and Ellora Caves), visiting ancient temples like Shirdi and Siddhivinayak, and heritage walks in Mumbai and Pune. Urban & Leisure: Strolling along Marine Drive, street food tours in Mumbai, and shopping in city malls.
