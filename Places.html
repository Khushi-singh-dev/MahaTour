Places to go in Maharashtra

Ajanta & Ellora Caves:

Explore the UNESCO World Heritage Ajanta and Ellora Caves, Elephanta Caves, and various forts like Raigad Fort and Pratapgad Fort.

Mumbai:

Visit the Tadoba-Andhari Tiger Reserve, Sanjay Gandhi National Park, or hill stations such as Mahabaleshwar, Lonavala, and Matheran.

Mahabaleshwar & Panchgani:

Plan visits to revered sites like the Shirdi Sai Baba Temple, Trimbakeshwar Temple, or the Ashtavinayaka temples.
